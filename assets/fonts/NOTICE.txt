Fonts used only to draw the Open Graph share cards (src/lib/seo/og-card.tsx).

They are static Latin-subset instances of the same Google Fonts the site already
self-hosts through next/font: Bricolage Grotesque (weights 500, 600, 700),
IBM Plex Sans (500) and IBM Plex Mono (500). next/og cannot read WOFF2 or
variable fonts, so these were converted to TTF with fontTools.
